The site sits around Beijing's 798 Art District, surrounded by three art parks, an art university, and residential blocks home to many art workers. The design begins from this condition — a desire to make a comfortable home that holds the distinct personality of different artists.

Because artists tend to transform their belongings, the project hands the lead back to the residents once the first phase is built. Three ways of transforming it are offered: repainting, planting, and building renovation. The second phase is designed under the residents' direction, and what emerges is an art community of vivid, mismatched character.
