Commissioned by Peking University, this teaching building draws on the formal language of the historic Peking University Red Building — abstracting and reinterpreting its brick facade to evoke continuity with the campus's architectural heritage. The program centers on an open, 24-hour academic environment: a cascading sequence of indoor and outdoor terraces, reading platforms, lecture halls, and informal gathering spaces that encourage spontaneous exchange between students and researchers.
